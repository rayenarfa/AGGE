# Google Form Blueprint: AGGE Website Identity & Operations

This blueprint is formatted to make copy-pasting the questionnaire into [Google Forms](https://forms.google.com) as simple and fast as possible. 

---

## Form Title
`AGGE Website Setup Questionnaire`

## Form Description
```text
This questionnaire will help us understand your vision, goals, and day-to-day needs for the new Association of Geosciences & Geo Engineering (AGGE) website. 

Please answer the questions below to guide the setup and design of your portal.
```

---

## SECTION 1: Brand & Vision
*Understanding who you are, who you want to reach, and how the site should look.*

### Question 1.1
* **Question Text:** What is the main mission of AGGE? (What is its ultimate goal?)
* **Question Type:** `Paragraph`
* **Help Text:** (Draft proposal: To support global collaboration, research, and technical training in geology, geophysics, and energy transition.)

### Question 1.2
* **Question Text:** Who are we building this website for?
* **Question Type:** `Checkboxes`
* **Options:**
  1. `University researchers and teachers`
  2. `Professional geologists, geophysicists, and engineers`
  3. `Students and young researchers`
  4. `Environmental and energy policy experts`
  5. `Corporate sponsors and energy companies`
  6. `Other` (Google Forms has a built-in "Add 'Other'" link)

### Question 1.3
* **Question Text:** Countries & Currencies
* **Question Type:** `Multiple Choice`
* **Options:**
  1. `Strictly Global: Support Euro (€) only and global contact methods.`
  2. `Multi-Currency: Support both Euro (€) and US Dollars ($).`
  3. `Other (Please describe)`

### Question 1.4
* **Question Text:** What core values are most important to AGGE? (e.g., technical excellence, protecting the environment, student mentoring, scientific innovation)
* **Question Type:** `Paragraph`

### Question 1.5
* **Question Text:** How should the website feel when someone visits?
* **Question Type:** `Multiple Choice`
* **Options:**
  1. `Modern & Technical (Sleek dark themes, digital mapping focus)`
  2. `Traditional & Academic (Classic institutional layouts)`
  3. `Clean & Eco-Friendly (Light cream tones, sustainability focus)`
  4. `Bold & Future-focused (Vibrant gradients, high contrast)`
  5. `Other`

### Question 1.6
* **Question Text:** Do you have a logo, brand colors, or style guides we must use?
* **Question Type:** `Paragraph`

### Question 1.7
* **Question Text:** Are there any partners, sponsors, or affiliates we should display on the website?
* **Question Type:** `Paragraph`

## SECTION 2: Main Goals & Purpose
*Identifying what you want visitors to do on your website.*

### Question 2.1
* **Question Text:** What are the top 3 most important goals for this website? (Rank them 1, 2, 3)
* **Question Type:** `Checkboxes`
* **Options:**
  1. `Get new people to sign up for memberships`
  2. `Get people to register for events (conferences, workshops, webinars)`
  3. `Show current geological projects around the world`
  4. `Sell training courses and classes`
  5. `Publish journals, newsletters, and articles`
  6. `Build local community chapters`

### Question 2.2
* **Question Text:** What is the absolute most important button on the homepage? (e.g., "Join Now", "See Events", "Contact Us")
* **Question Type:** `Short answer`

### Question 2.3
* **Question Text:** How will your team use this website in daily operations? (What manual work do you want this website to solve or automate for you?)
* **Question Type:** `Paragraph`

*Defining the main options and paths for users.*

### Question 3.1
* **Question Text:** Membership Options & Offerings: Please write down the plan names (e.g., Student, Individual Professional, Corporate), their prices, and what benefits the member gets for each:
