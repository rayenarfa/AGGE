## Environmental Policy

EAGE Events implement a waste-reduction policy addressing **Reduce, Reuse, Recycle**. Our objective is to research and prioritise the purchase of items that support the use of recycled materials, or that can be recycled after use.

## Programme & Catalogue and Event App

The Programme & Catalogue has been reduced in size to focus on the most essential information, therefore reducing paper usage. The Event App serves as a useful compliment/replacement and provides the most accurate version of the Technical Programme schedule in addition to networking and discussion functions.

## Lanyards & Badges

Our lanyards are made using recycled rPET material. The metal hooks and fabric will be sent to a local supplier for recycling after the event. We also provide recyclable name badges without a plastic pouch.

## Bags

Our handy lightweight bags are made from recycled cotton and designed ideally for everyday use. The bags are also circular, which means that they can be recycled again after use.

## Transport

We fully encourage the use of shared transport for our staff and delegates. Where possible, the conference accommodation options are offered within walking distance from the venue and nearby public transport stations.
