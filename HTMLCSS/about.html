About AGGE

The Association of Geosciences & Geo Engineering is dedicated to global excellence in earth sciences, geological engineering, and subsurface technologies.

Empowering the Global Geoscience Community

Founded to bridge the gap between academic geoscience and practical engineering applications, AGGE serves thousands of geoscientists, geophysicists, and geo-engineers worldwide.

We organize international conferences, publish peer-reviewed research, host continuing education modules, and provide student mentorship programs across 45+ regional chapters.

Our Core Values

  • Scientific Excellence: Rigorous peer-reviewed standards and empirical research integrity.
  • Global Inclusivity: Accessible knowledge sharing across developing and developed nations.
  • Environmental Stewardship: Responsible subsurface resource utilization and geological risk mitigation.
