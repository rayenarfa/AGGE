## Objective
Establish a secure, centralized administrative area for editors, managers, and system administrators to oversee the site.

## Requirements & Scope
1. **Access Control**:
   - Ensure the `/admin` prefix is guarded behind server-side and client-side role authorization checks.
2. **Dashboard Layout**:
   - Design a navigation shell with quick access tabs for Content (CMS), Events, Education, Memberships, Submissions, and Site Settings.
3. **Data Views & Management**:
   - Display dashboard statistics (active members, pending submissions, transaction summaries).
   - CRUD portal for managing registered site users and editing their roles.
   - Access to system `AuditLog` events.
